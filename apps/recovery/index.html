Recover your wallet

Stop every agent, or move everything out of your cyphos-guard smart account, with your recovery address. This page works on its own: no cyphos-guard service, no passkey. Your wallet signs, and your wallet sends the transaction.

1Connect your recovery wallet

The wallet whose address you entered as "recovery address" when you created the account (e.g. MetaMask).

2Your smart account

3Emergency stop (optional)

Removes your passkey from the account. Every permission your agents have, and every approval you signed with the passkey, stops working at once, even if the cyphos-guard service is down or compromised. Your funds stay in the account; your recovery wallet can still withdraw them below. Use this if you think an agent, its key or your passkey is compromised. Afterwards the passkey can't use this account any more.

x402 vaults are normally empty (they are topped up just before each payment); any rest in a vault can only go to the sellers you listed.

4Withdraw

Sends all USDC and ETH of the account (and any gas deposit it holds) to your recovery address. Your wallet asks you to sign, then to send the transaction; it pays a small network fee.

Base Sepolia testnet only. This page loads nothing from third parties; it reads the blockchain through your wallet. Agents of an empty account can't spend anything.
